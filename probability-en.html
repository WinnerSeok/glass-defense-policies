Drop Rates

Last updated: November 18, 2026 · game version 1.0

What you get

Each pull in the Workshop gives one shape card. Duplicates are used to level the card up.

Pulls cost blueprints (1 per pull, 10 for 11 pulls). Blueprints are earned by clearing stages or can be bought in the shop. You can also watch an ad for 1 free pull per day. Rates are the same for earned and purchased blueprints.

Pity

· If no Epic or better appears for 49 pulls in a row, pull 50 is guaranteed Epic or better (in the ratio of the rates above).

· If no Legendary or better appears for 99 pulls in a row, pull 100 is guaranteed Legendary (when one is available).

Locked cards

Some Legendary and Mythic cards only appear after you own the combo cards they are made from (see Condition). Until then, their share goes to the other available cards of the same grade; if a whole grade is locked, its rate is added to Common. The live rates are shown in the Workshop.
