Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Scope and contact

This policy explains how the Glass Defense app handles game data stored on your device, anonymous usage analytics, advertising, and support requests.

Contact: cheonseokwi@gmail.com

2. Game data stored on your device

Stage progress and stars, sand / blueprint piece / ember balances, card counts and levels, gacha pity counters, crusher records and settings (such as sound) are stored only on your device to run the game. There is no sign-up or login, and the app does not ask for your name, email or date of birth.

The app does not sync this saved data between devices. The anonymous usage analytics described in section 3 are sent to Google. You can delete it by clearing the app data or uninstalling the app.

3. Anonymous usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To improve the game and decide on updates, we collect usage statistics with Google Analytics for Firebase: stage start / clear / fail (stage number, wave, stars), gacha pulls and the best grade, whether a rewarded ad was watched, and crusher stage results. We do not send your name, email or anything that directly identifies you.

Firebase may automatically process an app instance ID (a random number created per install), device information such as model, OS version, language and country, and app usage time. Analytics data is configured not to be used for ad personalization.

This data may be processed by Google outside your country, including the United States, and is kept according to the Google Analytics data retention setting (14 months) and Google's policies. Clearing the app data or reinstalling creates a new app instance ID.

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Advertising

The app includes the Google AdMob SDK (a banner at the bottom of the game screen and optional rewarded ads). To request and show ads, Google may process your IP address, ad and app interactions, diagnostics, and device identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID, for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Your approximate location may be inferred from your IP address; the app does not request location permission.

This information may be sent to Google's servers and processed outside your country, including the United States, under Google's policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses information from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Where consent is required (e.g. the EEA and UK), Google's consent form is shown on first launch so you can choose about personalized ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

5. Payments

In-app items (blueprint piece packs and Remove Ads) are sold through Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled by Google Play; we never receive card numbers or passwords. To deliver items, prevent double delivery and handle refunds, the app stores the product, order ID, purchase token, purchase time and piece amounts on your device only.

6. Support requests

If you email us, we use your email address, message and any attachments you send only to reply. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information. Support records are deleted within 3 years after the request is resolved, unless the law requires otherwise. Support email is handled through Gmail.

7. Sharing

We do not sell support information or share it with unrelated third parties, except where required by law. Usage analytics are processed by Google Analytics for Firebase and ads are provided by Google AdMob.

8. Your rights and children

You may ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold. Game data that was never sent to us stays on your device, and you can delete it yourself by clearing the app data. The app does not ask for age and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and give notice of significant changes before they take effect.
